Why This Exists

Information is the starting point. Verification supports decisions.

A listing tells you what's for sale. An expat forum shares other people's experiences and opinions. Neither one checks the HOA's finances, confirms whether that addition was ever permitted, or tells you what daily life is actually like on that street. We do, independently, before you decide, not after.

Costa Rica has no title insurance. Due diligence is the buyer's only real protection, not a backup to some other safety net. There is also no national MLS and no statutory fiduciary duty, so the verification a US or Canadian buyer expects the system to handle is not handled by the system here. It falls to people. Good agents often do far more than the law asks of them, on their own time and at their own risk. That work should not have to rest on one person's word.

Buyers are routinely advised to seek independent representation. Few genuinely independent options exist. We work only for the buyer. No commission, no referral arrangements, and the same fixed fee whether the purchase goes ahead or you decide against it.

Every statement above is documented, with the statutes and government sources, in our free guide to buying and selling property in Costa Rica.

Central Valley, Costa Rica
On record

Under Law 7933 a condominium unit is the unit itself plus a share of the common areas, so reserves, arrears and special assessments sit with the association rather than on the title. The accountant’s fee statement is what shows whether fees are current. It is requested, not disclosed.

Client-supplied, and reported in full if we receive it. Requirements differ by canton: some municipalities issue one to any applicant holding the survey plan, others only to the registered owner.
