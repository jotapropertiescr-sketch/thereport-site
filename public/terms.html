Base scope. Every refresh includes a records re-check: title status, corporate standing, and municipal tax standing as they exist on the date the refresh is performed, presented against the prior findings so that changes are identifiable.

Optional additions. At the client's election, and at the additional fee stated at checkout, a refresh may also include any of the following: HOA or condominium financial health; permit and construction compliance; and a Site Visit/Condition Check. Where all three additions are selected, the bundled Full Refresh rate is applied automatically. A refresh covers only the base scope plus the additions actually purchased. Site Visit/Condition Check. A Site Visit/Condition Check purchased as part of a refresh carries the same limitations stated for the Full Edition in Section 2.
