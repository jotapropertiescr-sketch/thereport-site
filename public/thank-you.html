What happens next

  1. We confirm the property or zones you identified against the public record before any work begins.
  2. If anything needs clarifying, we write to you at the email address on your order. Watch for that message, since it is the most common reason a report takes longer than expected.
  3. Full Edition only: we contact you to arrange access for the site visit. Access coordination is the single biggest factor in your delivery date.
  4. Your finished report arrives by email as a PDF, with a delivery manifest listing every source consulted.

Delivery windows

Area Edition, 1 to 2 business days. Records Edition, 2 to 3 business days. Full Edition, 5 to 7 business days. Timelines begin once your order is confirmed. If a registry or municipal office is closed or slow to respond, we finish the work properly rather than deliver an incomplete report on schedule.
