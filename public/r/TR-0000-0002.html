01Scope and limitations

Edition: Records Edition  ·  Sections 3, 4 and 5 included  ·  Sections 6, 7, 8 and 9 not included

This report states what the public record said about the property named above, on the dates stated in each section. Every record was pulled for this engagement. Nothing has been reused from another file.

This is not a legal opinion and does not replace review by a licensed Costa Rican attorney. It is not a valuation, not an inspection, and not an endorsement of the property. It does not say whether the property is a good buy or suitable for any particular person. Where a section was not purchased or could not be completed, that is stated in the section rather than omitted, and the absence of a finding is not a finding that no issue exists.

Records change. A finding accurate on the date it was pulled may not describe the position today.

About the figures in this sampleValues, dates and names here are illustrative and have been redacted or replaced. An issued report carries the actual values, the statement each one came from, and the date that statement was pulled.

03Title and registry standing

Source: Registro Nacional, Bienes Inmuebles  ·  Checked 12 October 2026
Finding recorded

The registered owner on the date checked, the instrument under which title was acquired, and the date that instrument was recorded.

Charges appearing against the finca on the date checked: one mortgage in first rank in favour of a Costa Rican bank, recorded with its date and principal, and one annotation. Each is reported with the date it was entered and the party recorded. A charge is reported as a fact of the register. This report does not say what it means for a transaction, which is a question for the buyer's attorney.

Why this mattersAn annotation (anotación) is a marginal entry on the folio real. It can reflect a pending transfer, a judicial measure, or a correction in progress. It does not itself say which, and the underlying document has to be obtained separately to find out.

No usufruct, habitation right or recorded easement appeared against the finca on the date checked. That is stated as nothing appearing on that date, not as a guarantee that nothing exists.

04Corporate standing

Source: Registro Nacional, Personas Jurídicas  ·  Checked 13 October 2026
Reported

Title is held by a company. Reported here: the cédula jurídica, the company's status in the register, the legal representatives recorded and the powers held by each, and whether the company is current on its corporate filings and the annual corporate tax.

Whether a representative can sell the property alone or only jointly is a matter of the powers recorded. Those powers are reproduced as recorded.

Limit of this sectionBeneficial ownership is filed in the RTBF register, which is not publicly accessible. This report cannot state who ultimately owns the company behind the title, only what the public corporate record shows.

05Tax standing

Sources: Municipalidad de Santa Ana, Hacienda TRIBU-CR  ·  Checked 13 October 2026
Finding recorded

Municipal property tax account for the finca: the periods shown as paid, any period shown outstanding, the amount on the municipal statement, and the date of the statement relied on.

National obligations recorded against the holding company in TRIBU-CR, including the annual corporate tax and the status of the company's own filings.

Why this mattersUnpaid municipal periods attach to the property rather than to the person who incurred them. A buyer can inherit arrears recorded against a prior owner, which is why the statement date is given rather than a general statement that taxes are current.
