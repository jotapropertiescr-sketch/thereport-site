01Scope and limitations

Edition: Full Edition  ·  All sections included  ·  Site visit performed 09 October 2026

This report states what the public record said about the property named above, on the dates stated in each section, together with what was observed at the property on the date of the site visit. Every record was pulled for this engagement. Nothing has been reused from another file.

This is not a legal opinion and does not replace review by a licensed Costa Rican attorney. The condition snapshot is visual and observational only. It is not a home inspection and not an engineering assessment: no walls were opened, no systems were tested, and nothing is certified as structurally, electrically or hydraulically sound. A licensed inspector is engaged separately for that.

It is not a valuation and not an endorsement. It does not say whether the property is a good buy or suitable for any particular person. Records change, and a finding accurate on the date it was pulled may not describe the position today.

About the figures in this sampleValues, dates and names here are illustrative and have been redacted or replaced. An issued report carries the actual values, the statement each one came from, and the date that statement was pulled.

05Tax standing

Sources: Municipalidad de Santa Ana, Hacienda TRIBU-CR  ·  Checked 06 October 2026
Reported

Municipal property tax account for the filial: periods shown as paid, any period shown outstanding, the amount on the municipal statement and the date of the statement relied on. All periods were shown paid through the current quarter on the statement dated 06 October 2026.

Whether the solidarity tax on high-value homes applies at the registered value, and if so whether the declaration is current with Hacienda. The declared value, the threshold in force on the date checked, and the filing status are each stated.

Why this mattersUnpaid municipal periods attach to the property rather than to the person who incurred them, which is why the statement date is given rather than a general statement that taxes are current.

06HOA and condominium financial health

Source: Condominium administrator, requested under Ley 7933  ·  Received 08 October 2026
Finding recorded

Requested from the administrator and reported here: the current monthly quota for the filial, the condominium's operating position for the last full year, the balance held in the reserve fund, the number of filiales in arrears as a proportion of the total, and whether any extraordinary assessment (cuota extraordinaria) has been approved or is under discussion.

The account of the filial itself: whether the quota is current and whether any balance is outstanding against it. A balance outstanding against a filial follows the filial, not the seller.

Why this mattersA condominium with a thin reserve fund and a material proportion of filiales in arrears has a predictable future: either the common areas degrade or the remaining owners are assessed for the shortfall. Neither appears in a listing, and neither is visible from a walk-through.

Minutes of the most recent general assembly were requested. Where an administrator declines a request or does not respond within the delivery window, that is reported as what it is, and the section is marked not completed with the reason stated rather than left to look clean.

07Permit compliance

Sources: CFIA seal register, Municipal Control Constructivo  ·  Checked 08 October 2026
Finding recorded

Construction permits on file with the municipality for the filial, the CFIA seal recorded against each, and the professional registered as responsible. The built area shown on the permit file is compared against the area declared for tax and against the area in the registry.

In this sample those three figures do not agree. The discrepancy is reported with each figure and its source, and nothing is said about why they differ or what follows from it.

Why this mattersConstruction without a recorded permit is not a paperwork detail. It can block a future sale, obstruct financing, and expose an owner to a municipal order to legalise or demolish. The exposure transfers with the property.
Limit of this sectionFull CFIA plan sets are not publicly accessible. Seal validity is verifiable; the contents of the stamped plans are not, so this report cannot state whether what was built matches what was approved beyond what the municipal file itself records.

09Property condition snapshot

Direct observation at the property  ·  Attended 09 October 2026
Reported

What was visible on the date attended, recorded with photographs: the structure and its visible condition, roof and visible water ingress, visible cracking, drainage and the fall of the land, boundary walls and fences against the plano, and the state of the common areas and access.

Observations are recorded as observations. Nothing is rated, scored, or described as acceptable or unacceptable.

Limit of this sectionVisual and observational only. Walls were not opened, no electrical, water or drainage system was tested, and nothing is certified as structurally sound. Anything that warrants testing is noted so a licensed inspector can be engaged for it, which is a separate engagement THE REPORT does not perform.
